DIWALI FESTIVAL

About Diwali

Diwali is popularly known as the Festival of Lights. It is celebrated in many parts of India with lamps, decorations, sweets and family gatherings. People clean and decorate their homes, wear new clothes and exchange gifts with relatives and friends.

Main Celebrations

  • Lighting lamps
  • Decorating homes
  • Making Rangoli
  • Exchanging sweets and gifts
  • Family celebrations
