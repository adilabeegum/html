Vegetables are the edible parts of herbaceous plants—including roots, stems, leaves, bulbs, and tubers—that are typically prepared and consumed as part of a savory meal. Unlike fruits, which develop from the flowers of plants and contain seeds, vegetables are defined by their vegetative structures and culinary utility. They come in a vast range of vibrant colors and textures, serving as a foundational pillar of human nutrition. Incorporating them daily provides a powerhouse of health benefits, as they are exceptionally rich in dietary fiber, essential minerals, vitamins (like A, C, and K), and antioxidants that protect cellular health, aid digestion, and reduce the risk of chronic illnesses.
