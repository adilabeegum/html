HOLI FESTIVAL

About Holi

Holi is one of the most colourful festivals of India. It is popularly known as the Festival of Colours. People celebrate Holi by playing with colourful powders and water. The festival brings people together and represents joy, friendship and the arrival of spring.

Main Celebrations

  • Playing with colours
  • Traditional music
  • Dancing
  • Preparing traditional food
  • Meeting family and friends
