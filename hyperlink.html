FESTIVALS OF INDIA

India is a land of diverse cultures and traditions.Various festivals are celebrated throughout the country with great joy and enthusiasm.

1. Onam

Onam is a popular harvest festival celebrated mainly in Kerala.It is celebrated with great enthusiasm and includes traditional activities such as Pookalam, Onasadya, Vallam Kali and cultural programmes. Onam is associated with the legendary King Mahabali.

2. Diwali

Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, is one of the most widely celebrated festivals in India. People decorate their homes with lamps, exchange sweets and gifts, wear new clothes and enjoy various celebrations with family and friends.

3. Holi

Holi is known as the Festival of Colours. It is celebrated with colours, music and traditional food. People come together to celebrate the festival by applying colourful powders to one another. Holi represents happiness, friendship and the arrival of spring.
