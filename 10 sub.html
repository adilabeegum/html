About Us

MEA Engineering College is a self-financing engineering college located in Nellikunnu, Perinthalmanna, Malappuram, Kerala.

Departments

The college offers courses in Computer Science, Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Information Technology and other disciplines.

Vision and Mission

Vision

To provide quality education and develop competent, responsible and socially committed professionals.

Mission

To provide quality teaching, good infrastructure and opportunities for research and career development.
