About Onam

Onam is the vibrant ten-day harvest festival of Kerala that commemorates the annual homecoming of the legendary King Mahabali and marks the Malayalam New Year.Celebrated during the Malayalam month of Chingam, Onam unites people of all backgrounds in a shared expression of cultural pride and secular harmony. Homes come alive with Pookkalam, colorful floral arrangements created at doorsteps to welcome the benevolent king. Families wear new traditional attire, and the atmosphere fills with festive cheer, traditional folk songs, and community celebrations.The heart of the festival culminates on Thiruvonam with the Onam Sadhya, a grand vegetarian feast featuring dozens of traditional dishes served on a fresh banana leaf. Cultural displays like Vallam Kali (snake boat races) and Pulikali (tiger dance) highlight the rich heritage of the region, as noted by Kerala Tourism.
