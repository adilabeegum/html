About Us


MEA Engineering College is a self-financing engineering college located at Vengoor, near Perinthalmanna, in Malappuram district, Kerala. The college is committed to providing excellent and value-based education with a flair for ethics and professionalism. It is affiliated to APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, Kerala and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE). The college provides students with opportunities for academic learning, practical training, research and professional development in engineering and computer applications.

Courses Offered

MEA Engineering College offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in engineering and computer applications. The major undergraduate programmes include B.Tech. in Civil Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Information Technology, Mechanical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Electrical and Computer Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. The college also offers Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and Master of Computer Applications (MCA).
